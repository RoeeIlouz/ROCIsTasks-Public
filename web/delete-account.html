ROCIs Tasks

Delete your account and data

You can delete your ROCIs Tasks account and all the data linked to it at any time, directly in the app or by sending us a request.

Fastest

Delete it in the app

  1. Open ROCIs Tasks and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & GDPR.
  3. Tap Delete My Account & Data and confirm.

Your account and cloud data are deleted immediately.

Can't open the app?

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address you use to sign in, and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Email support@rocisapps.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in identity).
  • All tasks, subtasks, categories and settings stored in the cloud.
  • Your subscription status record in our database.

What isn't deleted

  • Data stored only on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Events in your device or Google calendar — the app only reads them.
  • Payment records kept by Google Play or Lemon Squeezy, which they retain as required by law.

Subscriptions are cancelled separately

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions) or through your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, so you're not charged again.

מחיקת חשבון

באפליקציה: הגדרות ← פרטיות ו-GDPR ← מחק את החשבון והנתונים שלי. אם אין לכם גישה לאפליקציה, שלחו מייל אל support@rocisapps.com מהכתובת שאיתה אתם מתחברים, ונמחק את החשבון והנתונים תוך 30 יום. מחיקת החשבון אינה מבטלת מנוי – יש לבטל אותו ב-Google Play.
